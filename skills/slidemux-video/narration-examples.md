# Narration examples

Spoken guide copy, sized to the clip. The slug names the recorded step.

## Good

| slug | clip | narration |
|------|------|-----------|
| `open-dashboard` | 4s | "Here's your dashboard — every project you're tracking, at a glance." |
| `create-project` | 6s | "Spin up a new project in one click; name it and you're ready to go." |
| `invite-teammate` | 8s | "Invite a teammate by email and they land right inside this workspace, no setup on their end." |
| `export-report` | 5s | "When you're done, export the whole thing as a shareable report." |

## Bad (and why)

| narration | problem |
|-----------|---------|
| "Dashboard. Button. Projects list. Menu." | Reads UI labels; not a spoken sentence. |
| "In this comprehensive overview of the primary dashboard interface, you can observe the complete enumeration of all currently tracked projects along with..." | Way over the ~2.3 words/sec budget for the clip; gets cut off. |
| "Welcome! Let me show you around." (on slide 4 of 7) | Intro copy on a middle slide; breaks the flow. |
| "Click the blue Create button in the top right." | Narrates the mouse, not the value. |
| "Saturday morning…" over a slide that says "The afternoon, already sorted." | Parallel script; voiceover must match the visible headline. |
